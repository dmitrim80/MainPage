Current Focus

Survey week on the Río Dulce

From 18–24 November 2026, a full inspection of Teki Malo: rigging survey, mechanical inspection, and complete condition survey. Staying at Bruno’s Hotel on the river.

Travel Route

  • Depart Newark (preferred) or Philadelphia
  • Fly to Guatemala City (La Aurora International)
  • Preferred: local flight Guatemala City → Puerto Barrios, then ~1.5-hour taxi into Río Dulce
  • Alternative: 6-hour bus (~$30) or taxi from Guatemala City
The river doesn’t just hold boats. It holds the next chapter of the search.

Bruno’s Hotel sits in the heart of the cruising community — open-air pavilion dining, pool under the palms, and docks right on the water. The aerial view of Lake Izabal and the Río Dulce makes the geography clear: protected water, jungle banks, and a direct path out to the Caribbean at Livingston.

If the survey confirms

  • Base in Río Dulce National Park / Lake Izabal (permit expected through ~April)
  • Cruise north from Livingston into Belize waters
  • Continue toward Mexico — Cancún / Yucatán coast
  • Target a mid-to-late March Gulf crossing toward Key West / Marathon
  • Overall aim of reaching Florida by May
