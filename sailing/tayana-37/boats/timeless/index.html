Description

Timeless is a 1983 Tayana Pilothouse 37 with two steering stations, including a sheltered helm inside the pilothouse. The seller reports a professionally installed 40 hp Yanmar diesel with 35 hours, a new mainsail, a resewn jib and recently reworked teak.
