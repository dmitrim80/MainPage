S/V Teki Malo — vessel overview

A 1988 Tayana 37 cutter designed by Robert Perry, Teki Malo is a Taiwan-built, double-ended, full-keel cruiser with solid fiberglass construction. The design is known for offshore cruising, traditional craftsmanship, and an all-teak interior.

The listing identifies her as hull #553 of a stated 588 boats and a two-owner vessel, with the current owners reporting 17 years of sailing and maintenance. They describe the survey conducted two years before the listing as having no recommendations and finding her “in excellent condition.” The survey date and report have not been independently verified here.
