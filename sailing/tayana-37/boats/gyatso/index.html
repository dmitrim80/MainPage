Description

Gyatso is a 1985 Tayana 37 cutter in Edgewater, Maryland. Robert Perry designed the fiberglass cruiser, and Ta-Yang Yacht Building Co. built it in Taiwan. The seller reports that all lines lead aft to the cockpit for shorthanded sailing.

The seller offered Gyatso as a restoration project. Reported work includes a rebuilt Yanmar diesel and sail and canvas upgrades by Quantum Sails Annapolis. Additional electrical and navigation equipment was included for future installation.

Equipment and accommodations

Sails, canvas and deck

  • Quantum Sails Annapolis supplied a mainsail and staysail in November 2022 and inspected the genoa and replaced its UV strip that month.
  • Fully enclosed Quantum Sails bimini and dodger from March 2022.
  • Fiberglass deck without teak decking.

Accommodations and galley

  • Butterfly companionway with the galley to starboard and navigation station to port.
  • Port dinette, starboard settee, port head and forward main cabin.
  • Force 10 two-burner gimbaled propane range, cooler/freezer and sink.

Electrical and power

  • Three 12 V, 206 Ah SOK LiFePO₄ house batteries, two solar panels and a 12 V wet-cell starter battery dated December 2022.
  • 12 V DC and 110 V AC systems.

Listed navigation and communications

  • VHF with AIS, SSB radio, satellite phone and satellite-ready AM/FM stereo.
  • Two GPS units, one integrated with a chartplotter, plus radar, autopilot, knot log, depth sounder and wind instrument.

Included refit equipment

  • Garmin electronics, Victron power management and Blue Sea Systems components for future installation.
