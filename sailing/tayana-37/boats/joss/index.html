Description

Joss is a 1983 Tayana 37 with a white fiberglass hull, a long keel and wheel steering. The wood-trimmed interior has two cabins and five berths. Listed cruising equipment includes solar power, radar, AIS and an autopilot.

Boat24 lists Joss as reserved. View the original Boat24 listing.
