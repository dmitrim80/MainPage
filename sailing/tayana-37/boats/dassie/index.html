Overview

Dassie is a full-keel Tayana 37 that returned from a winter cruise in the Bahamas before she was listed in Deltaville. The broker reported that the teak decks had been removed, the deck core repaired where needed, and the bowsprit repaired in 2020.

Equipment and refit

  • Engine and transmission. Perkins 4-108 diesel, with 542 hours reported since its 2019 rebuild. The broker also reported a transmission rebuild.
  • Rig and cockpit. Running rigging replaced in 2020, standing rigging in 2021, and a full canvas cockpit enclosure added in 2023.
  • Steering and navigation. Monitor windvane, Alpha 3000 autopilot, and Vesper AIS transceiver.
  • Power. Two 175 W Renogy solar panels and six 6 V house batteries installed in 2021.
  • Ground tackle. 20 kg Vulcan anchor with 230 ft of chain and a McMurray electric windlass.
  • Tankage. 90 gal diesel, 65 gal fresh water, and 25 gal holding.

Specifications, refit history, and photos are from the YaZu Yachting listing on Yachtr.
