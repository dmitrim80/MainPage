Description

Dassie is a 1980 Tayana 37 full-keel cutter documented in Deltaville, Virginia. She had recently returned from a winter cruise in the Bahamas. Her refit included rebuilding the Perkins diesel and transmission, removing the teak decking and repairing the deck core where needed, and repairing the bowsprit in 2020.

Running rigging was replaced in 2020, and standing rigging, solar panels, and the house battery bank were updated in 2021. A full cockpit enclosure was added in 2023. For cruising, Dassie carries a Monitor windvane, Alpha 3000 autopilot, Vesper AIS, two anchors with an electric windlass, and a Highfield tender with a Yamaha outboard.
