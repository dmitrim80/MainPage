Description

Josie is a 1988 Tayana 37 full-keel cutter documented in Deltaville, Virginia. YaZu Yachting reports a refit that included a partial engine rebuild. Her cruising equipment includes a Hydrovane, a Raymarine autopilot, a carbon-fiber hard bimini, and a 480 W solar array paired with a 600 Ah house bank.

Below deck, the U-shaped galley and navigation station flank the companionway. The forward cabin has a V-berth, and the head has a separate shower.

Equipment and systems

Accommodations

  • U-shaped galley to port and navigation station to starboard at the companionway

  • Forward V-berth and a port head with a separate shower

  • Victron MultiPlus II 3,000 W inverter (2021) and Victron charger (2025)

  • Webasto 12,000 BTU reverse-cycle air conditioning (2021)

Navigation and communications

  • Icom IC-424G VHF and Vesper Marine XB-8000 AIS (2023)
  • Iridium GO! with external antenna (2023)

Sails and rigging
