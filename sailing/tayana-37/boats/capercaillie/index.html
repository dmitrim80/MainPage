Sailing/Tayana 37/Capercaillie

1989 Tayana 37 Aft Cockpit Cutter

Capercaillie

Dunoon, Scotland

From Maine to Scotland

Capercaillie crossed the Atlantic from Maine to Scotland with her original owner and went on to cruise Scotland’s west coast. The owner kept the original build drawings. At the time of the listing, she had been in the same ownership since new.

Robert Perry designed the Tayana 37 for Will Eckert of Flying Dutchman Yachts. Ta Yang Yacht Building in Taiwan built nearly 600 of the design, primarily for the U.S. market. Capercaillie is the aft-cockpit cutter version.

The listing calls for polishing, fresh antifouling paint and renewed varnish.
