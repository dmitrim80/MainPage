Description

Flamboyant is a 1979 Tayana 37 cutter documented in Kemah, Texas. The double-ended fiberglass hull has a full keel and hung rudder. The seller reports a 2019 repower with a 30 hp Yanmar diesel, gearbox and shaft, followed by two new fuel tanks relocated amidships in 2021.
