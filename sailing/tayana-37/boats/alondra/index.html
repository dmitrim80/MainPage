Description

Alondra is a 1978 Tayana 37 cutter, hull No. 150, designed by Robert Perry. Brewer Yacht Sales listed her in Branford, Connecticut, and marks her as sold.

The broker records a Yanmar 4JH3E diesel and replacement fuel, water and holding tanks, installed in 2003. Listed equipment includes a 6.6 kW Northern Lights generator, two solar panels, a Raymarine autopilot and air conditioning.
