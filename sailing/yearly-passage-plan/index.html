01 · The loop

Move with the seasons,
not against them.

The course is organized around broad weather windows: south before the harshest winter, west through the islands, north before hurricane season builds, and back to Norfolk in autumn.

02 · Seasonal chapters

The year in four movements.

Each chapter has a different rhythm: offshore commitment, island hopping, a careful return north, then a summer coastal season.

01

Late October–December

East Coast → Bermuda → St. Martin

Stage in Hampton Roads, cross the Gulf Stream only in a stable multi-day window, clear at St. George’s, then use the Bermuda stop to reset before the 863 nm run south.

  • Norfolk to Bermuda · about 640 nm
  • Bermuda to St. Martin · about 863 nm
  • Provision the second leg for 10–12 days
Open passage chart ↗
02

December–February

Leewards, Virgins & Puerto Rico

Short hops around St. Martin, a westbound break to the BVI, then a slower January through the Virgins and Spanish Virgin Islands toward Puerto Rico.

Open passage chart ↗
03

February–early May

Puerto Rico → Bahamas → Florida

Work west along Puerto Rico, wait for a Mona Passage window, clear the Dominican Republic and Turks & Caicos, then climb the Bahamas before crossing to Florida.

Open passage chart ↗
04

May–November

Florida → Maine → Norfolk

Run north outside, use only Class A inlets, round Hatteras in a settled window, spend summer in New England and Maine, then work south to Norfolk before November.

  • Florida bailouts at Fort Pierce, Canaveral and St. Augustine
  • Cape Cod Canal with a fair current
  • September southbound from Maine

03 · Go / no-go gates

The calendar suggests.
The forecast decides.

  1. 01Gulf Stream crossings

    Recheck the current axis, wind direction and sea state immediately before departure.

  2. 02Cape Hatteras

    Use a settled window with enough margin for the full rounding—not only the first day.

  3. 03Mona Passage

    Wait for moderated trades and swell; account for tidal and current interaction.

  4. 04Clearance

    Confirm current entry, departure and reporting requirements for every jurisdiction.
