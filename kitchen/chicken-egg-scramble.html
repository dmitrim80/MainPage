Chicken & Egg Scramble

450 – 550 kcalHealth Score 90 🍃

Ingredients

Eggs · chicken · spinach · onion · tomatoes · cucumber · carrots

How to prepare

  1. Dice cooked chicken or cook fresh pieces in the pan.
  2. Add onion and spinach; cook until softened.
  3. Pour in beaten eggs and scramble together until set.
  4. Plate the scramble.
  5. Add tomato wedges, cucumber, and carrots on the side.
  6. Season to taste and serve warm.
