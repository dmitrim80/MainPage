Spinach & Avocado Bowl

380 – 480 kcalHealth Score 94 🍃

Ingredients

Spinach · avocado · tomatoes · cucumber · carrots · light grain

How to prepare

  1. Prepare a small portion of rice or grain if using.
  2. Wash and plate fresh spinach as the base.
  3. Add tomato wedges, avocado cubes, and cucumber.
  4. Place carrots on the side.
  5. Season lightly with salt, pepper, or a simple dressing.
  6. Serve fresh.
