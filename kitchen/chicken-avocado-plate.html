Chicken Avocado Plate

500 – 620 kcal Health Score 92 🍃

Ingredients

Chicken breast · avocado · spinach · cucumber · black beans · baby carrots

How to prepare

  1. Season chicken breast and grill or pan-sear until cooked through.
  2. Rest the chicken 2–3 minutes, then slice.
  3. Arrange a bed of fresh spinach on the plate.
  4. Add avocado cubes, cucumber rounds, and black beans.
  5. Place sliced chicken on top and add baby carrots.
  6. Finish with a light drizzle of oil or dressing.
