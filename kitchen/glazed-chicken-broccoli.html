Glazed Chicken & Broccoli

480 – 600 kcalHealth Score 90 🍃

Ingredients

Chicken · broccoli · tomatoes · baby carrots · glaze

How to prepare

  1. Season chicken and brush with a light glaze.
  2. Pan-sear or roast until cooked through and glazed.
  3. Steam broccoli until bright green and tender.
  4. Plate chicken with broccoli.
  5. Add tomato wedges and baby carrots.
  6. Serve warm.
