Yogurt & Fruit Parfait

280 – 380 kcalHealth Score 86 🍃

Ingredients

Yogurt · fruit · walnuts · cinnamon

How to prepare

  1. Spoon a layer of yogurt into a glass.
  2. Add a layer of fruit (fresh or canned).
  3. Repeat yogurt and fruit layers as desired.
  4. Top with chopped walnuts.
  5. Dust with cinnamon.
  6. Optional: light honey drizzle. Serve chilled.
