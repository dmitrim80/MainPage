Chicken, Broccoli & Carrots Plate

450 – 620 kcal Health Score 90 🍃

Ingredients

Chicken · broccoli · baby carrots · cherry tomatoes

How to prepare

  1. Season and roast the chicken until the skin is golden and the meat is cooked through.
  2. Slice extra white meat and place it beside the drumstick.
  3. Steam the broccoli until tender-crisp.
  4. Serve with baby carrots and cherry tomatoes. Season the carrots lightly with pepper.
  5. Serve immediately.
