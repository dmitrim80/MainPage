Chicken, Beans & Carrots

520 – 650 kcalHealth Score 88 🍃

Ingredients

Chicken · black beans · baby carrots · spinach · peppers · light seasoning

How to prepare

  1. Season chicken and sear in a pan until golden and cooked through.
  2. Warm black beans on the side.
  3. Steam or microwave baby carrots until tender.
  4. Optionally shred extra chicken with spinach and peppers in the same pan.
  5. Plate chicken, beans, and carrots together.
  6. Serve immediately while warm.
