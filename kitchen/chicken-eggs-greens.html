Chicken, Eggs & Greens

550 – 700 kcal Health Score 90 🍃

Ingredients

Chicken · eggs · spinach · yellow peppers · black beans · baby carrots

How to prepare

  1. Season and roast or pan-sear the chicken pieces until golden and cooked through.
  2. Scramble the eggs with spinach and yellow peppers.
  3. Warm the black beans.
  4. Arrange the chicken, egg scramble, black beans, and baby carrots on the plate.
  5. Serve warm.
