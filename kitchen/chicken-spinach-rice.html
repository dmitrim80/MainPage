Chicken, Spinach & Rice

520 – 640 kcalHealth Score 87 🍃

Ingredients

Chicken · spinach · onion · peppers · rice · cherry tomatoes

How to prepare

  1. Cook rice according to package directions.
  2. Season and cook chicken until done.
  3. Sauté onion and peppers, then wilt spinach in the same pan.
  4. Spoon rice onto the plate and top with chicken.
  5. Add the spinach and pepper mix over the chicken.
  6. Ring the plate with cherry tomatoes and serve.
